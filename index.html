FROM THE FOUNDER

Why I Built
PRASAD'S EDUHUB

I wanted to create a learning space where students can find useful exam preparation resources without unnecessary barriers or confusion.

“

I built PRASAD'S EDUHUB with a simple thought — preparation should be easier to access and easier to organize. Students preparing for UPSC, MPSC and Banking examinations often have to search across many different places for lectures, notes, practice questions and current affairs. I wanted to bring these things together in one simple platform.

This website is my small effort to make learning more accessible. There is no login, no subscription and no premium lock. My goal is to keep building EDUHUB into a useful platform that students can use throughout their preparation journey.

This is not just a website I created — it is a platform I want to keep improving with the needs and feedback of students.

— Prasad Sunil Salwe
Founder, PRASAD'S EDUHUB
